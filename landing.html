Useful details

Questions, answered.

Understand how access, broker connections and execution controls fit together.

Do I need a broker account to explore KINGBOT?

No. You can create an account and explore the platform. Broker connectivity is required for broker-based execution, and availability depends on supported account and market-data verification.

Does creating an account activate a trading bot?

No. Account creation does not activate a bot or authorize orders. Subscription entitlements, broker connection, execution mode and server-side risk checks apply independently.

Can I use a demo account before live trading?

The platform is designed to distinguish demo and live execution modes. Use demo first and only enable live execution after reviewing your broker connection, risk settings and applicable requirements.

Are trading profits guaranteed?

No. Trading involves substantial risk, including the possible loss of capital. Strategy descriptions and analytical outputs do not guarantee accuracy, profit or future performance.
