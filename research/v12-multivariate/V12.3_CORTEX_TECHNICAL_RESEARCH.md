# KINGBOT V12.3 — Cortex SMC and Technical-Pattern Research

## Objective

This research-only stage tests whether causal Smart Money Concepts (SMC), candlestick and classical chart-pattern features add measurable predictive information to the existing V12.2 multivariate Cortex sequence model. It does not change production inference, model storage, Render services, broker integrations, customer accounts or order execution.

## Why this is a separate experiment

V12.2 repaired the BTC history feed and compared four HOLD-aware loss objectives. Its completed report showed full-depth BTCUSDT proxy data (2,500 bars per requested series), but only one of five specialist pilots passed the development guard; that candidate was rejected on the untouched final segment. The hold-boosted objectives also demonstrated a risk of improving HOLD recall while reducing directional BUY/SELL performance. V12.3 therefore holds the training objective fixed and isolates feature value.

## Causal feature groups

### SMC (16 features)

- Swing highs/lows confirmed with a two-bar right-hand confirmation. The event is emitted only on the confirmation candle; it is never backfilled onto the historical pivot.
- Break of Structure (BOS) and Change of Character (CHoCH), based on a close crossing the latest confirmed swing level.
- Liquidity-sweep proxies: wick through a confirmed swing level followed by a close back inside.
- Equal-high/equal-low proxies using a fixed 0.30 ATR tolerance.
- Three-candle Fair Value Gap (FVG) proxies and normalized gap size.
- Order-block proxies: the last opposing candle broken by a displacement candle that confirms BOS/CHoCH.
- Premium/discount position inside a rolling 64-candle dealing range.

### Candlestick (12 features)

Doji, hammer, shooting star, bullish/bearish engulfing, inside/outside bar, bullish/bearish marubozu, body-to-range, upper-wick and lower-wick ratios.

### Classical chart patterns (17 features)

Confirmed double-top/double-bottom neckline breaks, head-and-shoulders/inverse head-and-shoulders neckline breaks, ascending/descending triangle breakouts, symmetrical triangle state, rising/falling wedge state, bull/bear flag breakouts, range position, range compression, 20-bar breakouts and retest proxies.

These are intentionally explicit OHLC definitions, not subjective chart annotations. Order blocks, liquidity and FVGs are price-only proxies: OHLC candles do not contain true order-book depth, dealer inventory, institutional orders or reliable volume-at-price data.

## Controlled feature ablation

Every specialist is evaluated on identical target rows, label timestamps, globally purged chronological folds, unchanged V12.2 cross-market context, Transformer budget and one shared matched V9 reference prediction set. Four predeclared variants are compared:

1. `baseline` — original 28 strategy-masked V12 target features.
2. `smc_only` — baseline plus 16 SMC features.
3. `technical_patterns_only` — baseline plus 12 candlestick and 17 chart-pattern features.
4. `full_cortex` — baseline plus all 45 new features.

Training objective is fixed to inverse-frequency weighted cross-entropy for this feature ablation. Target feature scaling is fitted on training rows only. Pattern features are computed from current and past closed candles only. Context tokens retain V12.2's availability-after-close rule.

The best eligible variant is selected on the 70%-85% development window only. It is frozen before the untouched 85%-100% final window. The final window is not evaluated when no variant passes the development guard.

## Acceptance criteria — unchanged

- Balanced accuracy >= 0.36.
- Macro-F1 >= 0.34.
- At least 30 final test samples.
- Nonzero SELL, HOLD and BUY recall.
- Final selection-score improvement >= 0.005 over the matched V9 baseline.

The development guard also requires the unchanged quality gate and delta >= -0.01. These thresholds are not weakened.

## Data and limitations

The workflow reuses the isolated V12.2 market fetcher with a 3,000-bar request target. It uses closed candles only. BTC history comes from Binance BTCUSDT and is a USDT-quoted proxy rather than an exact BTCUSD quote. No synthetic candles or forward-filled missing observations are allowed.

Pattern definitions are heuristics that require robustness testing across symbols, timeframes, sessions and volatility regimes. OHLC-derived patterns are not proof of order flow or institutional activity. A successful classification result is not proof of profitability.

## Release conditions

No model weights are saved, registered, promoted or deployed. Any candidate must still pass all-timeframe specialist validation, multiple walk-forward periods, regime-separated evaluation, execution-aware backtesting including spread/slippage/commission, and shadow evaluation before any release discussion.
