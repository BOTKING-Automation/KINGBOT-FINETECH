# KINGBOT V12.5 — Joint Direction-Confidence Abstention

## Why V12.5 exists

V12.4 completed its corrected experiment but did not produce a candidate that passed the final gate. Its two-stage actionability head selected approximately 91%-98% of development observations under the F1-selected action threshold, while directional accuracy among selected actionable rows was approximately 47%-51%. That is not sufficient evidence that the gate reliably avoids uncertain directional calls.

V12.5 tests whether abstention should depend on both actionability and directional conviction, rather than actionability alone.

## Predeclared comparators

All variants use V12.4's causal regime-aware target/context panels, identical chronological partitions, unchanged labels and a matched V9 core training set.

1. single_head_full_cortex: the three-class Transformer.
2. actionability_only_gate: V12.4's two-head architecture and actionability-only HOLD gate.
3. joint_direction_confidence_gate: the same two-head model and training run as variant 2, but a SELL/BUY prediction is accepted only when a joint confidence score clears a calibration-selected threshold.

The joint score is P(actionable) multiplied by the absolute distance of the conditional BUY probability from 50/50. It approaches zero when the actionability head favours HOLD or the direction head is near 50/50. It is a classification confidence score, not expected return, a trade probability guarantee or profitability estimate.

## Calibration rule

Temperature scaling for both heads and both abstention rules is fitted only on a chronological calibration slice inside the outer training partition. The joint threshold is selected by the unchanged 0.60 balanced-accuracy + 0.40 macro-F1 score on calibration data, subject to 10%-80% trade coverage and nonzero recall for all three labels. If no threshold satisfies the constraints, the model abstains on all observations. The evaluation fold is never passed to threshold selection.

The same two-head network weights and probabilities are used for both two-stage decision rules, isolating the decision-gate effect.

## Acceptance gates

Unchanged:
- Balanced accuracy >= 0.36.
- Macro-F1 >= 0.34.
- At least 30 test samples.
- Nonzero SELL, HOLD and BUY recall.
- Development score delta >= -0.01 before final evaluation.
- Final selection-score improvement >= 0.005 versus matched V9, plus all absolute quality gates.

Development remains 70%-85%. Final remains 85%-100% untouched unless a candidate passes development. Metrics include trade coverage, direction accuracy among selected observations, actionability precision/recall/F1, mean selected/abstained joint confidence, calibration error and per-regime breakdown.

## Data and limitations

The research runner reuses V12.3/V12.4's causal OHLC technical features and 18 regime features. Cross-market features use closed-bar as-of lookup. Market-history collection and ML bar normalization remain capped at 3,000 bars; actual provider coverage is included in the artifact.

## Research-only boundary

No production inference, persistence, promotion, Render settings, broker links, customer accounts, secrets, live/demo orders or execution logic is changed. A classification pass is not evidence of profitability. Any future candidate requires additional walk-forward periods, regime-specific tests, execution-aware spread/slippage/commission simulation, drawdown analysis and shadow evaluation.
