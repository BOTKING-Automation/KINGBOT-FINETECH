# KINGBOT V12.4 — Cortex Class-Balance and HOLD-Recall Research

## Why V12.4 exists

The completed V12.3 experiment (run `42374099018`, artifact `kingbot-v123-cortex-technical-research`) evaluated five specialist/timeframe pilots. Only Strategic/1h selected a development candidate. Its selected `full_cortex` variant passed development with balanced accuracy `0.3669`, macro-F1 `0.3658`, and a matched-V9 selection-score delta of `+0.0304`.

On the untouched final window, that candidate reached balanced accuracy `0.3422` and macro-F1 `0.3362` across `1,847` samples. It improved matched-V9 selection score by `+0.00947`, but missed the unchanged minimum balanced-accuracy gate of `0.36` and macro-F1 gate of `0.34`. Final HOLD recall was only `0.1339`. The final candidate was correctly rejected. The other four pilots did not clear development selection.

The V12.3 training loss already used inverse-frequency weighted cross-entropy, so V12.4 tests alternative class-imbalance treatments instead of repeating the same weighting unchanged or lowering any quality threshold.

## Research question

Can a less extreme class-weighting scheme or effective-number/focal loss improve out-of-sample SELL/HOLD/BUY balance—especially HOLD recall—while retaining the same V12.3 full-Cortex feature representation and beating the matched V9 baseline?

## Controlled experiment

The only experimental dimension is the training loss profile:

1. `inverse_frequency_ce` — exact V12.3 loss control.
2. `sqrt_inverse_frequency_ce` — square-root inverse-frequency weights, intended to reduce over-correction from a rare class.
3. `effective_number_ce` — class-balanced effective-number weights (`beta=0.999`).
4. `effective_number_focal` — the same effective-number class weights with focal modulation (`gamma=1.5`).

All class weights are computed from training-fold labels only. Every profile uses the same full-Cortex features, model architecture, six-bar ATR labels, 12-epoch budget, AdamW configuration, common random seed, timestamp alignment, label purging and training-only feature scaling.

Each of the five V12.3 specialist/timeframe pilots is evaluated on the development window. Only profiles passing the unchanged development quality gates and development score-delta guard are eligible. Highest development selection score wins; HOLD recall is only a tie-breaker. The selected profile is frozen before the untouched final window is evaluated, and only that one profile is run on final data.

## Acceptance gates — unchanged

- Balanced accuracy at least `0.36`.
- Macro-F1 at least `0.34`.
- At least `30` untouched final samples.
- Nonzero SELL, HOLD and BUY recall.
- Final selection score at least `0.005` above the matched V9 score.

CI success means code/tests passed; it is not model acceptance. No candidate passes by virtue of a development score alone.

## Data, cost and isolation

The workflow fetches closed historical candles with the existing public Deriv feed and public Binance BTCUSDT history path. It does not require a customer's Deriv account, paid TradingView alerts, paid webhook services or new data subscriptions.

This is an isolated research branch based on the V12.3 research head. V9/V10 benchmark code, production inference, model registry/persistence, Render services, broker connections, risk controls and order execution are not changed. No V12.4 weights are saved, promoted or deployed.
